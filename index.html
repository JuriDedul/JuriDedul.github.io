`Lil Background

I'm a graduate of TalTech, where I studied Integrated Engineering in an English-taught programme. During my studies, I took part in Erasmus+ exchanges in Poland and the Czech Republic, including a semester at Czech Technical University in Prague. Outside university, I've volunteered at Vaba Lava Narva and Tallinn Music Week, helping organise events, and even performed in a historical production at Narva Castle.

I've always enjoyed exploring different fields, meeting new people and getting involved in things beyond my usual routine.
